Cookies & tracking

Cookies policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

When you browse the website

Our own website code does not set cookies or use local storage or session storage. We do not install analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts, images and scripts are served with the website; social media appears as ordinary links.

The hosting provider, GitHub Pages, receives the technical requests needed to deliver the website. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Your verification choice

To send an inquiry through the form, select Enable verification to load hCaptcha. Before you make that choice, our page does not load the hCaptcha script, create its frames or connect to it. You can choose Email instead and contact contactus.aestheticapparels@gmail.com without enabling verification.

Once enabled, hCaptcha may process IP address, device, browser and interaction information, and use security cookies or similar storage to distinguish people from automated abuse. The storage and its duration can vary with the provider’s configuration and your interaction. See hCaptcha’s privacy and cookie information.

Turn verification off

Use Turn off verification & reload in the inquiry form. Reloading stops the loaded verification code and clears the form. Verification will be off again until you choose to enable it. Your choice is not saved between page loads. This does not delete information already processed by the provider or cookies already stored; use your browser’s privacy settings to review or remove those.

Sending an inquiry

When you press Send inquiry after validation and verification, the form sends the necessary inquiry details and verification token to Web3Forms. The provider handles form delivery and anti-spam processing under its privacy policy. Read our privacy policy for more about inquiry information.

External links and the clipboard

If you select a social or certification link, the destination website may set its own cookies. The Copy website link button writes our public website address to your clipboard only when selected.

Future changes

If optional analytics or advertising services are added, we will update this notice and provide consent controls where required before those services run. For questions, contact contactus.aestheticapparels@gmail.com.
