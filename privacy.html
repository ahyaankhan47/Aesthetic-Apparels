Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

Who we are

Aesthetic Apparels provides garment sourcing services to clients worldwide. For privacy questions, business correspondence or requests about your information, contact contactus.aestheticapparels@gmail.com.

Information you provide

Our inquiry form asks for your name, email address and sourcing requirements. Company, product, estimated quantity and timeline are optional. Please avoid passwords, payment details, identity documents and other sensitive information. We do not add campaign tags or the current page address to your inquiry.

How your inquiry is handled

When you send an inquiry, the details you enter and your verification token are submitted to Web3Forms for processing and email delivery to our business. We use inquiry details to understand your requirements, communicate with you and discuss sourcing services. The privacy acknowledgement on the form concerns that inquiry; it is not a marketing subscription.

Before sending, you can use Clear form to remove the values entered on the page. Browser autofill is controlled by your browser. Sending information by email instead is subject to your email provider’s handling as well as ours.

Website and security information

GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, for hosting and security. Fonts, images and website scripts are served with the site. We do not install analytics or advertising trackers.

hCaptcha verification loads only after you select Enable verification. It can process IP, device and interaction information and may use security cookies or similar storage. You can choose Email instead, or stop verification on the current page using Turn off verification & reload. Reloading clears the form; it does not undo information already processed or remove third-party cookies. See the cookies policy.

Service providers and international processing

Our website uses GitHub Pages for hosting, Web3Forms for form processing, hCaptcha for verification and Gmail for business email. These providers may process information in countries other than yours under their applicable terms. Information needed to discuss your sourcing requirements may also be included in business correspondence.

Read the GitHub privacy statement, Web3Forms privacy policy, hCaptcha privacy policy and Google privacy policy.

How long information is kept

Inquiries sent through the form may be retained in the form provider’s systems and our business email. Web3Forms’ current policy describes retention for up to three years, subject to its terms, plan settings and deletion. Its retention period does not determine how long copies remain in business correspondence. Contact contactus.aestheticapparels@gmail.com to ask about information relating to your inquiry or request its deletion. Records connected with an ongoing business relationship, disputes or legal requirements may need to be retained.

Your questions and rights

Depending on the law that applies, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal information, to object to certain processing, or to withdraw consent where processing relies on it. You may also be able to complain to your local data-protection authority. Send requests to contactus.aestheticapparels@gmail.com; we may need enough information to identify the relevant inquiry and verify the request.

Security and external links

The published website and form submission endpoint use HTTPS. Please do not send confidential credentials or payment information through an ordinary inquiry. No internet service is completely risk-free. Links to social networks, certification organizations and other websites lead to services with their own privacy practices.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when website features or processing arrangements change. The date above identifies the current version.
